# The entropy-vs-T anomaly — artifact or effect?

> **EXPLORATORY — post-verdict re-analysis of recorded data. Not verdict-bearing; the registered record is closed. Generated by `engine/gen_post_verdict.py` from the event store; regenerable.**

**What this is:** the registered P5-4 secondary showed round-1 choice
entropy *falling* with temperature (0.906 → 0.787 → 0.777 bits), the
opposite of the naive expectation. This decomposes it.

## How the entropy was computed (registered secondary)

Adjudicator (`phase5_closeout_adjudicate.py`): pooled round-1 action
counts across **every completed valid run at each T**, Shannon entropy
`-Σ p·log2 p` over the two actions. Crucially, the pooled T=0.7 count
includes the full 96-cell tier-A lattice (16 personas × 6 cells) plus all
T=0.7 sweep and sentinel lanes, while T∈{1.0, 1.3} contain **only** the
sweep subset (4 personas + bare, 3 cells). The pooled statistic compares
different unit mixes — a composition (Simpson-type) confound by
construction.

## Verdict on the anomaly

**The decline survives matched-unit decomposition**: on the identical unit set entropy still falls from T=0.7 to higher temperatures. Aggregation explains part of the pooled magnitude but not the direction — the anomaly is real at the sweep-lattice level and remains an open observation (mechanism scope-sealed to future work).
