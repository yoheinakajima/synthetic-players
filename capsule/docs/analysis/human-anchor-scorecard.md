## Reading

The scorecard's arc: the bare subject failed every human anchor except the
community-framing interior point (#7). The persona layer moves pool-level
aggregates toward human levels (#1, #3) without reproducing the human
*mechanisms* — the δ-gradient stays absent at pool level (#2), and the swap
"refusals" (#6) are mechanism-ambiguous. The single mechanism-level match in
five phases is p13's δ-slope (#5). Level-matching is cheap; response-matching
is what failed — a one-line summary of why synthetic panels cannot yet
substitute for human ones where identification rides on incentive response.
