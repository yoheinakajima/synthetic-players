# Completion-amendment pattern

**Rule:** when a sealed registration leaves definitions incomplete, complete
them via a *prospective, outcome-blind completion amendment*: derive every
choice only from (a) sealed material, (b) a contemporaneously recorded family
analogy, (c) already-frozen in-tree semantics, or (d) a cited canonical
default. Show rejected alternatives with consequences; leave genuinely free
bits as explicit operator sign-off choices (never resolve them silently);
hand-work fixtures for every behavioral case and make them the engine
selftests verbatim.

**Why:** this preserved confirmatory status for four underspecified Family F
adversary arms (architect ruling: "confirmatory under completion amendment"
— vs demotion to exploratory). The spec-to-fixture-to-selftest chain closes
the spec/implementation gap that produced repeated earlier anomalies. A free
bit resolved by the operator pre-data *on a stated design rationale* (not
convenience) stays defensible in the report.

**How to apply:** any time a frozen protocol needs a definition filled in
after sealing: no peeking at collected data or live-model behavior, no
pilots; document original registration vs completion entry separately; have
an independent review rule per-item on confirmatory status. Design
non-degeneracy (a reading that collapses two registered arms into duplicates
must be wrong) is a legitimate sealed-material argument.
