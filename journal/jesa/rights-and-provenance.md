The candidate reuses only author-generated five vector figures and source-owned manuscript/analysis/instruction records already public at the immutable baseline. It includes no third-party paper PDFs, publisher figures, screenshots, private author answers, headshots, or editorial correspondence. References and narrow summaries attribute the external literature; the candidate does not republish external full text. The journal packet is a draft source PR, not a new journal/public-archive release.
