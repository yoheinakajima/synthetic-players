# Round 1 — multi-review synthesis

> **SOURCE STATUS:** Living summary of the first external manuscript-review round. The original reviews were supplied in conversation and are summarized here so the public review archive covers the full revision history. This file is not sealed experimental evidence.

## Review panel and common conclusions

The first circulation received adversarial feedback from multiple AI reviewers. The reviews agreed that the event-sourced, mechanically adjudicated pipeline was the paper's strongest credibility layer, but they also agreed that the manuscript was trying to carry too many empirical and methodological stories at once.

Common recommendations adopted later included:

- keep the core levels-versus-response result and the representation experiments;
- treat auditability as a credibility layer rather than the only empirical novelty;
- narrow claims about lightweight trait-persona conditioning rather than all synthetic participants;
- retain the precommitted discussion as a visible artifact while writing a conventional current discussion around it;
- disclose the fragile P5-1a threshold margin rather than hiding it;
- distinguish the human author from the agentic pipeline as research apparatus;
- move entropy, adversary-suite, and several cross-vendor findings out of the main arc.

## Major defects identified

The reviews raised five issues that drove the subsequent redesign:

1. **P5-3 multiplicity.** A per-candidate test with an existence claim firing on any of sixteen personas did not control familywise error. This led to the post-adjudication family audit and ultimately to p13's demotion from finding to replication target.
2. **Human-comparator mismatch.** The Dal Bó–Fréchette values came from different continuation probabilities, payoffs, incentives, assignment, and experience. “δ-matched” and N-fold language were removed.
3. **Unproven human microstructure.** The draft could establish nonidentification of microstructure from aggregate moments, but not that humans in the relevant game were necessarily interior while LLM prompts were corner-typed.
4. **Occupied broad thesis.** Concurrent work already showed that descriptive realism and treatment-effect accuracy can diverge. The paper needed to lead with its specific fixed-panel composition and representation mechanism.
5. **Three-papers problem.** The manuscript was asked to choose one empirical claim, one mechanism, and one credibility layer, with other findings moved to appendices or future papers.

## Resulting disposition

The core contribution became a fixed-panel strategic-interaction example in which coarse marginal checks and large between-prompt dispersion coexist with small, imprecisely estimated observed incentive-response contrasts. Later rounds supplied dependence-aware inference, the exact condition-mean identity, latent-person-invariance caveats, and the public correction architecture.
