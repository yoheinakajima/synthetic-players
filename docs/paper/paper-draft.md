## Abstract

Large language models are increasingly used as synthetic research participants, but they are often validated by whether their marginal responses resemble published human data. We report a five-phase prospectively registered program using a fixed panel of sixteen lightweight persona-conditioned GPT-4.1 configurations in strategic games. The panel passed preregistered broad-reference checks for condition-level cooperation in three of four repeated-game cells and produced large observed between-persona dispersion, yet changing continuation probability from 0.10 to 0.90 changed aggregate round-one cooperation by at most 0.08. Most persona–condition cells exhibited no observed within-cell variation, so the aggregate spread arose primarily across prompt configurations concentrated near behavioral corners. Separate representation experiments showed that one continuation sentence shifted cooperation from 0/40 to 37/40 on held-out episodes and that semantic action labels could override payoff dominance in a registered conflict condition. These findings identify a corner-mixture failure mode: coarse marginal checks can be satisfied while response to the experimental lever remains weak. All claims were prospectively registered and mechanically adjudicated from an event-sourced record; one registered inference rule was later downgraded after external review exposed missing family-level error control. Human references are published and protocol-nonmatched, the confirmatory results concern one fixed model–persona panel, and the registered round-one intervals used seat-level trials nested within episodes; we therefore do not claim human substitutability or final cluster-robust inference until the prespecified submission sensitivities are complete.

## 1. Introduction

Human behavioral experiments are slow and expensive; LLM calls are fast and nearly free. A growing literature reports that suitably conditioned LLMs produce data resembling human data — "algorithmic fidelity" [Argyle et al. 2023], "homo silicus" [Horton 2023], behavior "statistically indistinguishable from a random human" [Mei et al. 2024] — and a flagship formalization of *statistical realism* as the validation target now exists [PNAS 2026, realism framework]. Nearly all of this evidence validates at the level of marginals: means, distributions, aggregate replication.

Recent work shows that descriptive realism and causal fidelity can diverge at scale [Li & Ji 2026], that treating LLM outcomes as surrogates for human outcomes requires assumptions that marginal equivalence does not supply [Persson et al. 2026], and that intervention prompts can shift the model's implied latent user even when the explicit persona is held fixed [Lin et al. 2026]. Our contribution is not the divergence itself but a controlled mechanism-level decomposition in strategic interaction: **a commonly used lightweight persona construction passes coarse marginal checks while the incentive comparative static stays weak, and the observed condition-specific distributions exhibit a measurable mixture of empirically corner-concentrated prompt configurations.** Sealed templates and matched procedures rule out accidental changes in explicit persona assignment, environment randomization, and execution. They do not establish latent-person invariance across interventions; Lin-style user drift and the observed corner-mixture pattern can coexist.

The failure pattern has formal ancestors worth naming, as analogues rather than identities. In econometrics it is the reduced-form/structural distinction sharpened by the **Lucas critique** [Lucas 1976]: a fit to aggregates holds until the policy lever moves, because the fitted parameters were never structural — here, δ is the lever, and persona conditioning is the reduced-form fit. In psychometrics it is **construct validity** [Cronbach & Meehl 1955]: a measure is validated by behaving correctly across its nomological network, with dose-response as validity evidence a marginal cannot supply and **assay sensitivity** [ICH E10; Temple & Ellenberg 2000] naming the precondition our interiority gate enforces. In causal inference it is the **surrogate paradox** [Prentice 1989; VanderWeele 2013], now formalized for LLM outcomes [surrogacy framework 2026]. (Agent-based modeling's *equifinality* is the same trap at the aggregate-pattern level [Windrum et al. 2007; Grimm et al. 2005].)

**Contributions.** (1) The core result: a fixed persona panel passes preregistered coarse marginal checks while its δ comparative static stays ≤0.08 (§4.1). (2) The mechanism and identification analysis: broad condition-level bands only partially identify an aggregate treatment contrast; marginal means and variances do not identify the allocation of variation, distributional shape, or cross-condition coupling of prompt-indexed response surfaces (§4.2). (3) A decomposed map of control-channel interactions: single-sentence switches, token–payoff conflicts, and the persona presence/direction dissociation (§4.3). (4) A working reliability protocol — procedurally exact, extensively auditable, inferentially fallible — including a post-adjudication downgrade initiated by external review (§4.4, §6). A single persona-level slope is retained only as a replication target, not as a contribution.

## 2. Related work

**Occupied territory, and where we sit.** Li & Ji [2026] establish, across 59,508 participants and 11 interventions, that descriptive fit and intervention-effect accuracy can diverge and that prompt refinements improving realism do not reliably improve effect accuracy. Persson, Schultzberg & Ankargren [2026] formalize when LLM outcomes can serve as causal surrogates and why novel interventions still require human evidence. Lin et al. [2026] show that intervention prompts can change the implicit simulated population even when explicit personas are fixed. Persona-collapse and state-versus-trait work further shows that persona-conditioned populations can be structurally hollow or misallocate variation [Xiao et al. 2026; related state/trait work]. Our differentiation is narrower: in incentive-bearing strategic interaction, under prospective registration, we decompose a fixed explicit persona panel into between-prompt dispersion, within-prompt variation, and incentive response, identifying one concrete corner-mixture pattern that can conceal weak comparative statics. This pattern is complementary to, not exclusive of, latent-user drift.

**LLM strategic behavior (direct collisions).** Akata et al. [2025] characterize LLM repeated-game play, modulable by prompt interventions; Pal, Mallela, Hilbe, Pracher, Wei, Fu, Schnell & Nowak [2026] elicit strategies of five models in repeated PD varying δ, payoffs, horizon knowledge, and framing, finding no model fully consistent; counterfactual-reasoning evaluations find label perturbations mild but payoff perturbations exposing canonical-strategy preservation [arXiv:2603.19167]; "strategic robustness" is defined as payoff-preserving invariance [arXiv:2607.19670]. These study bare single models. Our distinct combination is a persona panel, a between/within decomposition tied to an incentive manipulation, prospective registration, and mechanical adjudication. The prompt results should therefore be read as evidence about representation-conditioned policies, not as proof that the base model possesses or lacks a representation-general strategic trait.

**Silicon-sampling critique lineage.** Bisbee et al. [2024] — our closest ally — find level match with compressed variance, distorted coefficients, and temporal drift in survey settings; Boelaert et al. [2025] report excess homogeneity; Anthis et al. [2025] catalog challenges; Hullman et al. [2026] argue for statistical calibration for confirmatory use (complementary: we address what must be *measured*). Park et al. [2024] show rich interview conditioning achieving strong individual-level replication — the strongest live candidate for a conditioning scheme that might pass response tests (Appendix A.5 Q6).

**Prompt sensitivity, personas, mechanism.** Format and phrasing sensitivity [Sclar et al. 2024; Brittlebench 2026], role-play framing [Shanahan et al. 2023], persona collapse [Xiao et al. 2026], and reviews of persona-experiment transparency [Batzner et al. 2025] all caution against treating a persona string as a stable human analogue. Work contrasting state and trait variation is adjacent to our between/within decomposition, but our data do not establish a human microstructural mismatch. RLHF-related diversity reduction is a plausible mechanism for corner concentration, not a result identified by this design. Full annotated map: `docs/analysis/literature-map.md`; differentiation table: `docs/analysis/novelty-relationships.md`.

## 3. The instrument

Primary deployment: gpt-4.1 (requested model identifier rechecked at each phase entry), temperature 0.7, 16-token outputs, and a fixed minimal behavioral-subject prompt with no game-theory vocabulary or reasoning scaffold. Phase 5 prepends one sealed persona sentence to byte-identical task text, giving every persona cell an exact bare twin. Cross-vendor tier: gemini-2.5-flash, descriptive; the original candidate, claude-haiku-4-5, failed the registered behavioral entry gate and was replaced under an archived amendment. All environment randomness is seeded; provider-side generation was not claimed to be seeded. Every call is archived with its rendered prompt, completion, decoding parameters, and provenance, making 4,576/4,576 recorded observations byte-exact replayable with zero live calls. Claims are structured predicates registered before their evidence and mechanically adjudicated in a fixed vocabulary. The registered two-sided interiority rule used Clopper–Pearson bounds on seat-level round-one trials and rejected floors and ceilings; because two seats are nested within one episode, episode-clustered sensitivity is a submission requirement rather than an accomplished property of the historical verdict. Under-specifications discovered at adjudication were resolved by outcome-blind completions. Behavioral sentinels supervised both endpoints, and the Phase 5 discussion was selected from precommitted branches sealed before dispatch.

The boundary of what this machinery guarantees, stated plainly: **the pipeline can enforce a registered predicate exactly; it cannot guarantee that the predicate represents a valid estimand, test family, or construct.** §4.4's family-error episode is the demonstration; it is not evidence that every registered inferential rule was valid. Scale: ≈30,500 recorded subject calls, ≈13M input tokens, ≈US$15 of model spend at list prices; zero invalid outputs in Phases 4–5 (0/1,712 in Phase 5, including temperature 1.3).

**Sample hierarchy and estimands.** deployment → persona prompt → condition → episode → seat → round → call; the confirmatory unit for Phase 5 is the complete persona sentence (names, ages, occupations are uncontrolled semantic treatments); n = 16 at the persona level. Two estimands are distinguished throughout: the conditional finite-panel estimand (these sixteen fixed prompts) and the persona-population estimand, for which n = 16 is small and all population-level language is correspondingly hedged. ## 4. Results

### 4.1 The core result: coarse marginal checks pass; the incentive comparative static stays weak

The preregistered leaning rule (≥2 of {agreeable, patient, risk-averse}) separates round-one cooperation by 0.5–0.7 in every non-swap cell. Because names, ages, occupations, and all three trait descriptors vary together, this is a property of the complete persona sentences, not a causal estimate for any individual trait. Pool means (0.349–0.505 across the four repeated cells) enter the published cooperation band in three of four cells. Raw observed between-persona SDs are 0.4241–0.4800, compared with published nonmatched human SDs of 0.3116 and 0.4122. By the historical registered criteria — broad band entry and raw variance comparability, evaluated per condition — the fixed panel passes. Those raw SD comparisons include finite-opportunity estimation noise and are not presented as latent-variance equivalence.

Across the δ manipulation, the pool's round-1 cooperation moves ≤0.08 in every cell family. The published comparator [Dal Bó & Fréchette 2011] — **a nonmatched comparator**: different δ values (0.50/0.75 vs our 0.10/0.90), different stage payoffs, monetary incentives, and subjects who accumulate experience across supergames, where our episodes are memory-less first exposures — moves by ≈0.42 in its pooled, experienced data, and *reverses* ordering in first-supergame play. We therefore make no matched-magnitude claim ("N-fold" language is retired); the load-bearing claim is internal: **the same pool that passes the registered marginal criteria fails to produce more than a small δ comparative static under matched, sealed, seed-controlled conditions.** A reanalysis of the DF replication microdata (first-exposure vs experienced views; per-subject endpoint mass; opportunity-count-matched variability) is scripted and pending the package download; its role is contextualization — the human per-subject response Δᵢ is unobservable in their between-session design, an asymmetry we state rather than paper over.

Interiority under the registered seat-level gate: 14 of 96 persona–condition cells are interior; ten of sixteen persona prompts are corner-classified in every registered cell; every corner-classified cell has zero observed within-cell variation. The restricted P5-1a fraction was 3/32 = 0.094 against θ₁ = 0.10, passing by one unit. We therefore treat the census and continuous rates as primary and the binary verdict as historical. Episode-clustered reclassification is a submission blocker because the two seat decisions are nested within episodes.

### 4.2 The mechanism, and what marginal checks cannot see

The panel's broad aggregate spread is generated primarily by between-prompt composition of empirically corner-concentrated policies. Let \(p_i(d)=E[Y\mid i,d]\), where \(i\) indexes the complete persona prompt and \(d\) the experimental condition. The full statements are in `docs/analysis/propositions.md`.

**Proposition A (partial identification of the aggregate contrast).** If a synthetic mean is accepted within tolerance \(\epsilon_d\) of a reference mean in each condition, then the error in the synthetic treatment contrast is bounded only by \(\epsilon_0+\epsilon_1\). Equivalently, separate accepted bands \([\ell_0,u_0]\) and \([\ell_1,u_1]\) imply only \(\Delta^S\in[\ell_1-u_0,u_1-\ell_0]\). Exact condition-specific mean matching would force the aggregate effect by identity; broad bands may leave zero, the wrong sign, or a materially attenuated effect admissible.

**Proposition B (microstructure and coupling).** Mean and total variance do not identify how variation is allocated between prompt configurations and repeated draws, nor do they identify distributional shape or corner mass. Even if the condition-specific distributions of \(p_i(d)\) were known, their marginals would not identify the cross-condition coupling and therefore would not identify the distribution of prompt-indexed responses \(\Delta_i=p_i(1)-p_i(0)\) without pairing or structural assumptions. This experiment pairs explicit persona prompts across conditions and can estimate prompt-indexed contrasts; interpreting those pairs as stable latent people additionally requires a latent-person invariance assumption that we do not test.

The study therefore identifies an observed corner-mixture pattern in one fixed prompt panel. It does not establish that human participants have a different microstructure, that RLHF caused the pattern, or that latent-user drift is absent.

### 4.3 Control-channel interactions

The bare subject: round-1 cooperation 0.000 at every δ (320 decisions; policy-level CP upper bounds ≈0.14–0.17); a single continuation sentence (S2, localized by a span-ladder and confirmed on fresh seeds) flips it to 0.925 (37/40); the identical wording factor is null in one-shot play (+0.006) — switches conduct only where context gives them referents. In the label-swap conflict cell (canonical payoffs, "Cooperate" on the defecting action), the bare subject chooses the cooperation-worded option 0/40 — taking the strictly dominated action whenever it carries "Defect" — while responding to matrix changes when labels do not oppose them (+0.788). The precise claim is therefore **conditional dominance**: semantic cues can override payoff dominance in conflict cells; payoff sensitivity is representation-conditional, not absent.

Personas add two separable forces. *Direction* drives the 0.5–0.7 leaning gaps. *Presence* flips the lexical attractor in the swap cell: all sixteen personas — both leanings, refusal shares 0.683–1.000 — choose the cooperation-worded/payoff-dominant option where the bare subject chose the opposite 40/40. Because word and payoff coincide there, the mechanism (incentive response vs prosocial-word attraction) is unidentified; the de-confounding cell (balanced payoffs × persona) is scope-sealed to future work, and this figure never appears without this caveat. The registered dominance predicate returned persona-dominant (pooled task-consistent share 0.128, CP [0.104, 0.155]; the authors' registered "task-text dominates" failed) — but the pooled figure is carried by the confounded swap cell; the confound-free repeated subset runs 0.234 task-consistent ("mixed" at the pinned bound), and the cleanest payoff-opposed slice (cooperative-leaning personas vs S2-absent wording *and* the dominant strategy) is 65% persona-consistent. Identity contests and usually beats the task layer; formal dominance holds only pooled. We describe these as **control-channel interactions**, not a hierarchy: precedence is cell-dependent.

### 4.4 A preregistered candidate and an externally identified inference defect

Persona p13 produced the largest gate-passing positive slope under the historical registered rule: 0.333 at δ=.10 and 0.750 at δ=.90, with a per-candidate one-sided lower bound of +0.083. The rule tested multiple persona × wording candidates and fired on any pass without declared family-level error control. A post-adjudication audit enumerated 96 registered-eligible and 32 evaluable clause-(a) surfaces; clause (b) was audited separately. Its initial 2,000-permutation max-statistic analysis returned \(p=0.0525\) with Monte Carlo SE 0.0050, which is too imprecise and too close to the threshold for a final confirmatory classification. The historical mechanical verdict remains unchanged, but p13 is treated only as a preregistered replication target pending a higher-precision family audit using a statistic reproducible under the full gate-and-selection procedure.

The scientifically important result here is the correction process: external review identified that exact enforcement of a registered rule did not make the rule familywise-valid. The episode is recorded as the program's first post-adjudication inferential downgrade, distinct from the twelve predictions refuted by new data. Trait interpretations of p13 remain exploratory because the complete persona sentence—name, age, occupation, and traits—is the manipulated unit.

### 4.5 Auditability (concise)

Twelve registered author predictions refuted and published; one post-adjudication downgrade from external review; four outcome-blind completions; a two-sided gate that blocked a false ceiling conclusion; sentinels that detected a time-indexed behavioral discontinuity in an unversioned endpoint mid-study (erosion, apparent recovery later exposed as the outlier, stable lower plateau) with a monitoring gap caught, disclosed, quarantine-ruled, and closed by an attestation gate; and a public, anonymously verified replay of all 4,576 recorded observations. The claim is deliberately bounded: the machinery is procedurally exact and extensively auditable, and its principal achievement is preserving enough provenance for outsiders to identify exactly where procedural correctness stopped short of statistical validity.

## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 What follows

For synthetic-participant practice: coarse marginal fidelity is a reduced-form fit, achievable by a one-sentence identity prepend, and its failure mode is silent — a researcher using this pool would have passed broad published cooperation bands and concluded that the shadow of the future barely matters, with clean-looking data. Validation should move to response surfaces: preregistered comparative-static predicates against pinned (and explicitly matched-or-not) human quantities, through assay-sensitivity gates, per (model, prompt registry, provider route, time-window) — the last coordinate demonstrated real by the drift record. Statistical calibration [Hullman et al. 2026] and surrogacy assumptions [2026] are complementary formal routes; Lin et al.'s [2026] drift diagnostics address a potentially overlapping failure: explicit prompt identity does not guarantee latent-person invariance across interventions. For deployment: strategic behavior rewritable by one sentence or one identity line, with context-dependent channel precedence, is a safety-relevant property demonstrated rather than argued.

## 6. Limitations

Confirmatory results rest on one model deployment and a fixed panel of sixteen complete persona prompts; persona-population generalization is weakly identified. The historical round-one gates and several Clopper–Pearson bounds count seat decisions even though two seats are nested within an episode; episode-clustered sensitivity is required before submission and may change fragile classifications such as P5-1a. Raw between-persona SDs include finite-opportunity estimation noise and require a hierarchical or bias-corrected sensitivity before being compared as latent heterogeneity. The p13 family audit used only 2,000 permutations and an alternative lower-bound statistic, so it is not final. Explicit persona strings are paired across conditions, but latent-person invariance is not tested; Lin-style user drift may coexist with the observed corner-mixture pattern. The clause-(b) mechanism remains word/payoff-confounded, the human comparator is published and protocol-nonmatched, and the DF microdata reanalysis is contextual rather than a path to a matched design. P5-1a passed by one unit. The entropy-versus-temperature observation lacks an identified mechanism, the δ × high-temperature interaction is non-estimable under the registered design, and Gemini-tier results are descriptive under documented endpoint non-stationarity.
