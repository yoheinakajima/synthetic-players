## Abstract

Synthetic research participants built from large language models are typically validated by resemblance: do their outputs pass marginal checks — means inside published human bands, variance comparable to human panels? We report a five-phase prospectively registered program (≈30,500 recorded LLM decisions; every claim a machine-checkable predicate registered before data and adjudicated mechanically) showing that such coarse marginal validation can be passed by construction while the property experiments exist to measure — response to an incentive manipulation — remains weak. A pool of sixteen seeded trait-persona subjects (gpt-4.1) enters published human cooperation bands in three of four repeated prisoner's dilemma cells and matches or exceeds human between-subject variance, yet moves round-1 cooperation by at most 0.08 across a continuation-probability manipulation, against a published (nonmatched) human comparator whose corresponding movement is several times larger. We identify one concrete mechanism by which lightweight trait-persona pooling conceals this divergence: individual persona subjects are empirically corner-concentrated (zero observed within-cell variation), so pool variance is a bimodal mixture that coarse marginal statistics cannot distinguish from human interior heterogeneity; we state the two identification propositions this instantiates. We map the control channels that do govern behavior — a single continuation sentence flips bare-subject cooperation 0.000→0.925 (37/40 held-out); a payoff-dominated option is chosen 40/40 when labeled "Defect"; persona presence and persona direction dissociate — and report a suggestive existence result: one persona ("competitive, patient, risk-averse, 61") passed an interiority-gated discount-factor-slope assay, with family-level permutation p = 0.0525 ± 0.0050 over the complete registered decision family. The authors' registered predictions were refuted twelve times, and one registered inference rule was downgraded post-adjudication after external review identified a family-error defect; all of it is published and replayable. We argue coarse marginal fidelity is the synthetic-subject analogue of a reduced-form fit, and that substitution claims should be evaluated on response surfaces, per (model, prompt registry, provider route, time-window), using preregistered batteries of this kind.

## 1. Introduction

Human behavioral experiments are slow and expensive; LLM calls are fast and nearly free. A growing literature reports that suitably conditioned LLMs produce data resembling human data — "algorithmic fidelity" [Argyle et al. 2023], "homo silicus" [Horton 2023], behavior "statistically indistinguishable from a random human" [Mei et al. 2024] — and a flagship formalization of *statistical realism* as the validation target now exists [PNAS 2026, realism framework]. Nearly all of this evidence validates at the level of marginals: means, distributions, aggregate replication.

Recent work has begun to show that descriptive realism and causal fidelity can diverge at scale [Li & Ji 2026], that treating LLM outcomes as surrogates for human outcomes requires assumptions that marginal equivalence does not supply [surrogacy framework 2026], and that intervention prompts can shift the implied population, converting simulated experiments into observational studies [Lin et al. 2026]. Our contribution is not the divergence itself but a mechanism and a demonstration: **a widely used construction — lightweight trait-persona pooling — passes coarse marginal checks while the incentive comparative static stays weak, and it does so through a specific, measurable composition: a mixture of empirically corner-concentrated subjects.** Because our conditions are sealed, byte-diffed templates under matched procedures, intervention-induced population drift [Lin et al. 2026] is ruled out as the explanation; the mixture composition carries it.

The failure pattern has formal ancestors worth naming, as analogues rather than identities. In econometrics it is the reduced-form/structural distinction sharpened by the **Lucas critique** [Lucas 1976]: a fit to aggregates holds until the policy lever moves, because the fitted parameters were never structural — here, δ is the lever, and persona conditioning is the reduced-form fit. In psychometrics it is **construct validity** [Cronbach & Meehl 1955]: a measure is validated by behaving correctly across its nomological network, with dose-response as validity evidence a marginal cannot supply and **assay sensitivity** [ICH E10; Temple & Ellenberg 2000] naming the precondition our interiority gate enforces. In causal inference it is the **surrogate paradox** [Prentice 1989; VanderWeele 2013], now formalized for LLM outcomes [surrogacy framework 2026]. (Agent-based modeling's *equifinality* is the same trap at the aggregate-pattern level [Windrum et al. 2007; Grimm et al. 2005].)

**Contributions.** (1) The core result: a persona pool passes coarse marginal checks (band entry; variance comparability) while the δ comparative static stays ≤0.08 (§4.1). (2) The mechanism and two identification propositions: coarse marginal validation does not identify aggregate response (Proposition A — and only *coarse* criteria are cheat-able, since exact condition-specific mean matching would force the aggregate effect by the identity Δ = μ₁ − μ₀); aggregate moments do not identify within/between allocation or the per-subject response coupling Δᵢ (Proposition B) (§4.2). (3) A decomposed map of control-channel interactions: single-sentence switches, token–payoff conflicts, and the persona presence/direction dissociation (§4.3). (4) A suggestive, fully audited existence result at the family level (§4.4). (5) A working reliability protocol — procedurally exact, extensively auditable, inferentially fallible — including a post-adjudication downgrade initiated by external review (§4.5, §6).

## 2. Related work

**Occupied territory, and where we sit.** Li & Ji [2026] establish, across 59,508 participants and 11 interventions, that LLM simulations can look right while causal effects go wrong, and that prompting refinements improving realism do not reliably improve effect accuracy. The surrogacy framework [2026] formalizes the assumptions under which LLM-outcome effects recover human effects, noting that prompted models yield "overly peaky or stereotyped" response profiles — independent corroboration of our corner concentration. Lin et al. [2026] show a distinct failure: identical personas drift into different implicit populations across treatment conditions. Our differentiation: we isolate one concrete concealment mechanism (corner-mixture composition of a fixed pool) in incentive-bearing strategic interaction, under prospective registration, with the drift mechanism excluded by design.

**LLM strategic behavior (direct collisions).** Akata et al. [2025] characterize LLM repeated-game play, modulable by prompt interventions; Pal, Mallela, Hilbe, Pracher, Wei, Fu, Schnell & Nowak [2026] elicit strategies of five models in repeated PD varying δ, payoffs, horizon knowledge, and framing, finding no model fully consistent; counterfactual-reasoning evaluations find label perturbations mild but payoff perturbations exposing canonical-strategy preservation [arXiv:2603.19167]; "strategic robustness" is defined as payoff-preserving invariance [arXiv:2607.19670]. These study bare single models. None construct persona pools, decompose marginal fidelity from response, run interiority-gated assays with family-level audits, or operate under mechanical adjudication — and our results qualify "modulable by prompts": prompts move marginals readily; audited incentive transmission appeared at suggestive strength exactly once.

**Silicon-sampling critique lineage.** Bisbee et al. [2024] — our closest ally — find level match with compressed variance, distorted coefficients, and temporal drift in survey settings; Boelaert et al. [2025] report excess homogeneity; Anthis et al. [2025] catalog challenges; Hullman et al. [2026] argue for statistical calibration for confirmatory use (complementary: we address what must be *measured*). Park et al. [2024] show rich interview conditioning achieving strong individual-level replication — the strongest live candidate for a conditioning scheme that might pass response tests (Appendix A.5 Q6).

**Prompt sensitivity, personas, mechanism.** Format/phrasing sensitivity [Sclar et al. 2024; Brittlebench 2026]; role-play framing [Shanahan et al. 2023]; persona caricature/collapse [Lutz et al. 2025; arXiv:2604.24698]; LMs behaving trait-fixed (ICC≈1) where humans show ICC≈0.27 [arXiv:2601.15395] — our zero within-cell variation is that assumption made flesh; human traits as density distributions of states [Fleeson 2001] versus point-masses; RLHF diversity reduction as the expected cause of the determinism [Kirk et al. 2024; Casper et al. 2023]. Full annotated map: `docs/analysis/literature-map.md`; differentiation table: `docs/analysis/novelty-relationships.md`.

## 3. The instrument

Subjects: gpt-4.1 (revision-pinned, verified at each phase entry), temperature 0.7, 16-token outputs, a fixed minimal behavioral-subject prompt (no game-theory vocabulary, no reasoning scaffold); Phase 5 prepends one sealed persona sentence per subject to byte-identical task text, giving every persona cell an exact bare twin. Cross-vendor tier: gemini-2.5-flash, descriptive; the original candidate, claude-haiku-4-5, **failed the registered behavioral entry gate** and was replaced under a registered, archived amendment — the failed round's full report is preserved in-repo. All environment randomness is seeded; every call is an event (hashed prompt, completion, decoding params, provenance), making the program byte-exact replayable with zero live calls (4,576/4,576 observations; verified anonymously post-publication). Claims are structured predicates registered before data, mechanically adjudicated in a fixed vocabulary; a two-sided interiority gate (episode-level CP95 wholly inside (0.05, 0.95)) licenses slope inference and rejects floors *and* ceilings — a rule that prevented a false "δ-flat at ceiling" conclusion in Phase 4. Under-specifications discovered at adjudication were resolved by outcome-blind completions (four instances, each generating a new freeze-lint check). Behavioral drift sentinels with sealed baselines supervised both endpoints. The Phase 5 discussion was written as four pre-committed branches sealed before dispatch, with a registered selection rule.

The boundary of what this machinery guarantees, stated plainly: **the pipeline can enforce a registered predicate exactly; it cannot guarantee that the predicate represents a valid estimand, test family, or construct.** §4.4's family-error episode is the demonstration. Scale: ≈30,500 recorded subject calls, ≈13M input tokens, ≈US$15 of model spend at list prices; zero invalid outputs in Phases 4–5 (0/1,712 in Phase 5, including temperature 1.3).

**Sample hierarchy and estimands.** deployment → persona prompt → condition → episode → seat → round → call; the confirmatory unit for Phase 5 is the complete persona sentence (names, ages, occupations are uncontrolled semantic treatments); n = 16 at the persona level. Two estimands are distinguished throughout: the conditional finite-panel estimand (these sixteen fixed prompts) and the persona-population estimand, for which n = 16 is small and all population-level language is correspondingly hedged. ## 4. Results

### 4.1 The core result: coarse marginal checks pass; the incentive comparative static stays weak

The trait-assigned leaning rule (≥2 of {agreeable, patient, risk-averse}) predicts round-1 cooperation with 0.5–0.7 gaps in every non-swap cell — persona direction is behaviorally enormous. Pool means (0.349–0.505 across the four repeated cells) enter the published human cooperation band in three of four cells; between-persona SDs (0.4241–0.4800) match or exceed the published human between-subject SDs (0.3116 and 0.4122). By the registered coarse criteria — band entry and variance comparability, evaluated per condition — the pool passes.

Across the δ manipulation, the pool's round-1 cooperation moves ≤0.08 in every cell family. The published comparator [Dal Bó & Fréchette 2011] — **a nonmatched comparator**: different δ values (0.50/0.75 vs our 0.10/0.90), different stage payoffs, monetary incentives, and subjects who accumulate experience across supergames, where our episodes are memory-less first exposures — moves by ≈0.42 in its pooled, experienced data, and *reverses* ordering in first-supergame play. We therefore make no matched-magnitude claim ("N-fold" language is retired); the load-bearing claim is internal: **the same pool that passes the registered marginal criteria fails to produce more than a small δ comparative static under matched, sealed, seed-controlled conditions.** A reanalysis of the DF replication microdata (first-exposure vs experienced views; per-subject endpoint mass; opportunity-count-matched variability) is scripted and pending the package download; its role is contextualization — the human per-subject response Δᵢ is unobservable in their between-session design, an asymmetry we state rather than paper over.

Interiority: 14 of 96 persona-cells are interior; ten of sixteen personas are pure-corner everywhere; every corner cell shows zero within-cell variation across all decisions. The registered mixture predicate P5-1a passed at 3/32 = 0.094 against θ₁ = 0.10 — by one unit; we treat the continuous quantities (the census, the within-cell variation, the distributional comparisons) as primary and the binary verdict as secondary.

### 4.2 The mechanism, and what marginal checks cannot see

The pool's human-like spread is a bimodal mixture of empirically corner-concentrated subjects. Two propositions (full statements and the binary-outcome variance decomposition μ(d), B(d), W(d), Δᵢ in `docs/analysis/propositions.md`):

**Proposition A (what the study demonstrates).** Coarse marginal validation — band entry, pooled variance comparability, per-condition tolerances — does not identify aggregate treatment response. The qualifier is load-bearing: by Δ = μ₁ − μ₀, *exact* condition-specific mean matching would force the correct aggregate effect; only coarse criteria are cheat-able, and coarse criteria are what the literature uses.

**Proposition B (identification).** Even exact aggregate moments do not identify the between/within allocation, the corner concentration, or the joint coupling of potential outcomes across conditions (the Δᵢ distribution) without repeated-measures or structural assumptions. Human panels and corner mixtures can be moment-equivalent; only within-subject designs or response-surface tests separate them.

The corner concentration itself is expected, not anomalous: RLHF reduces output diversity toward mode collapse [Kirk et al. 2024], and independent work reports "overly peaky or stereotyped" prompted response profiles in behavioral games [surrogacy framework 2026]. What our design adds is the demonstration that pooling such subjects *manufactures* the marginal signatures validators check.

### 4.3 Control-channel interactions

The bare subject: round-1 cooperation 0.000 at every δ (320 decisions; policy-level CP upper bounds ≈0.14–0.17); a single continuation sentence (S2, localized by a span-ladder and confirmed on fresh seeds) flips it to 0.925 (37/40); the identical wording factor is null in one-shot play (+0.006) — switches conduct only where context gives them referents. In the label-swap conflict cell (canonical payoffs, "Cooperate" on the defecting action), the bare subject chooses the cooperation-worded option 0/40 — taking the strictly dominated action whenever it carries "Defect" — while responding to matrix changes when labels do not oppose them (+0.788). The precise claim is therefore **conditional dominance**: semantic cues can override payoff dominance in conflict cells; payoff sensitivity is representation-conditional, not absent.

Personas add two separable forces. *Direction* drives the 0.5–0.7 leaning gaps. *Presence* flips the lexical attractor in the swap cell: all sixteen personas — both leanings, refusal shares 0.683–1.000 — choose the cooperation-worded/payoff-dominant option where the bare subject chose the opposite 40/40. Because word and payoff coincide there, the mechanism (incentive response vs prosocial-word attraction) is unidentified; the de-confounding cell (balanced payoffs × persona) is scope-sealed to future work, and this figure never appears without this caveat. The registered dominance predicate returned persona-dominant (pooled task-consistent share 0.128, CP [0.104, 0.155]; the authors' registered "task-text dominates" failed) — but the pooled figure is carried by the confounded swap cell; the confound-free repeated subset runs 0.234 task-consistent ("mixed" at the pinned bound), and the cleanest payoff-opposed slice (cooperative-leaning personas vs S2-absent wording *and* the dominant strategy) is 65% persona-consistent. Identity contests and usually beats the task layer; formal dominance holds only pooled. We describe these as **control-channel interactions**, not a hierarchy: precedence is cell-dependent.

### 4.4 A suggestive existence result, audited at the family level

One persona passed the registered interiority-gated slope assay: **p13** ("You are Harper, a 61-year-old landscape gardener. People who know you describe you as competitive, patient, and risk-averse."), interior at both S2-absent δ levels (0.333 → 0.750), one-sided 95% slope LB +0.083 — the program's only registered incentive-transmission pass in ≈30,500 decisions. External review identified that the registered clause tested each candidate at per-candidate α with the existence claim firing on any pass — no family-level error control was declared at freeze. The post-adjudication audit (full enumeration: 144 registered-eligible surfaces, 32 evaluable; joint gate+slope permutation with T_max over the family; B = 2,000, seeded) yields **p = 0.0525 ± 0.0050**, with three gate-passing candidates observed (p04/s2p, p05/s2a, p13/s2a). Status: **suggestive, not confirmatory** — the registered mechanical verdict stands as history; the inferential interpretation is downgraded, disclosed as the program's first post-adjudication claim-status downgrade (a category distinct from the twelve data-refuted predictions), and a new rule is registered going forward: every predicate must declare its family-level error control at freeze.

Why it remains the program's most interesting object: patience is the discount-factor trait [Falk et al. 2018]; the boundary p-value sits on a real effect direction with the *only* interior δ-pair in the design; and p05 — identical trait factors, age 35 — armed the same assay and failed the slope, while interior cells concentrate in factor-tension personas (competitive + risk-averse). All exploratory, n = 1 per point, with names/ages/occupations as uncontrolled treatments; the confirmatory content is a suggestive lead and a pre-specified replication target, nothing more.

### 4.5 Auditability (concise)

Twelve registered author predictions refuted and published; one post-adjudication downgrade from external review; four outcome-blind completions; a two-sided gate that blocked a false ceiling conclusion; sentinels that detected a time-indexed behavioral discontinuity in an unversioned endpoint mid-study (erosion, apparent recovery later exposed as the outlier, stable lower plateau) with a monitoring gap caught, disclosed, quarantine-ruled, and closed by an attestation gate; and a public, anonymously verified replay of all 4,576 recorded observations. The claim is deliberately bounded: the machinery is procedurally exact and extensively auditable, and its principal achievement is preserving enough provenance for outsiders to identify exactly where procedural correctness stopped short of statistical validity.

## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 What follows

For synthetic-participant practice: coarse marginal fidelity is a reduced-form fit, achievable by a one-sentence identity prepend, and its failure mode is silent — a researcher using this pool would have replicated human cooperation levels and concluded the shadow of the future barely matters, with clean-looking data. Validation should move to response surfaces: preregistered comparative-static predicates against pinned (and explicitly matched-or-not) human quantities, through assay-sensitivity gates, per (model, prompt registry, provider route, time-window) — the last coordinate demonstrated real by the drift record. Statistical calibration [Hullman et al. 2026] and surrogacy assumptions [2026] are complementary formal routes; Lin et al.'s [2026] drift diagnostics address a third, independent failure. For deployment: strategic behavior rewritable by one sentence or one identity line, with context-dependent channel precedence, is a safety-relevant property demonstrated rather than argued.

## 6. Limitations

Confirmatory results rest on one model deployment; n = 16 at the persona level; the persona-population estimand is weakly powered by design. The P5-3 mechanism is unidentified for 15/16 passes (word/payoff confound; de-confounding cell unrun by scope-seal). The family-error defect in the registered clause-(a) rule was caught by external review, not the linter — semantic and inferential completeness are not fully mechanizable, and the instance ledger (22 process instances → 6 rules) documents preregistration-under-automation degrading gracefully rather than perfectly. The human comparator is published and nonmatched; the microdata reanalysis is pending; human Δᵢ is unobservable in the comparator's between-session design regardless. P5-1a passed by one unit. The entropy-vs-temperature inversion (supplement) lacks a mechanism. The δ × high-temperature interaction is non-estimable by registered aliasing. Gemini-tier results are descriptive under documented endpoint non-stationarity.
