## Abstract

Synthetic research participants built from large language models are typically validated by resemblance: do their outputs pass marginal checks — means inside published human bands, variance comparable to human panels? We report a prospectively registered five-phase program (≈30,500 recorded seat–round decisions; every confirmatory claim a machine-checkable predicate registered before data and adjudicated mechanically from an event-sourced record outsiders can replay) on a fixed panel of sixteen lightweight trait-persona GPT-4.1 configurations in strategic games. The panel passes the registered coarse checks — condition-level cooperation inside published human bands in three of four repeated-game cells, with broad between-persona dispersion — yet moving continuation probability from 0.10 to 0.90 shifts aggregate round-1 cooperation by at most 0.08. Most persona–condition cells show zero within-cell variation, so the pool's dispersion arises primarily across prompt configurations concentrated at behavioral corners: a corner-mixture composition that coarse marginal statistics cannot see past (two identification statements make this precise). Separate representation experiments map the channels that do govern behavior: a single continuation sentence moves bare-subject round-1 cooperation from 0.000 to 0.925 (37/40 on held-out seeds), and semantic action labels override payoff dominance in a registered conflict cell (a payoff-dominated option chosen 40/40 when labeled "Defect"). These findings identify a failure mode for synthetic-subject validation: coarse marginal checks can be satisfied by mixture composition while response to the experimental lever stays weak. Human references are not protocol-matched, results concern one fixed model–persona panel, and we claim no human substitutability; we argue substitution claims should be evaluated on response surfaces, per (model, prompt registry, provider route, time-window), with preregistered batteries of this kind.

## 1. Introduction

Human behavioral experiments are slow and expensive; LLM calls are fast and nearly free. A growing literature reports that suitably conditioned LLMs produce data resembling human data — "algorithmic fidelity" [Argyle et al. 2023], "homo silicus" [Horton 2023], behavior "statistically indistinguishable from a random human" [Mei et al. 2024] — and a flagship formalization of *statistical realism* as the validation target now exists [PNAS 2026, realism framework]. Much of this evidence validates at the level of marginals: means, distributions, aggregate replication.

Recent work has begun to show that descriptive realism and causal fidelity can diverge at scale [Li & Ji 2026], that treating LLM outcomes as surrogates for human outcomes requires assumptions that marginal equivalence does not supply [surrogacy framework 2026], and that intervention prompts can shift the implied population, converting simulated experiments into observational studies [Lin et al. 2026]. Our contribution is not the divergence itself but a mechanism and a demonstration: **a commonly used lightweight construction — trait-persona pooling [Batzner et al. 2025, "Whose Personae?", a 63-study review] — passes coarse marginal checks while the incentive comparative static stays weak, and the observed condition-specific distributions exhibit a specific, measurable composition: a mixture of empirically corner-concentrated subjects.** Our design rules out changes in explicit persona assignment, execution procedure, and environment randomization across conditions (sealed, byte-diffed templates; matched seeds); it does not test latent-person invariance, so intervention-induced drift in the model's implicit completion of a fixed persona [Lin et al. 2026] is not excluded and may coexist with the composition we measure directly (§2, §6).

The failure pattern has formal ancestors worth naming, as analogues rather than identities. In econometrics it is the reduced-form/structural distinction sharpened by the **Lucas critique** [Lucas 1976]: a fit to aggregates holds until the policy lever moves, because the fitted parameters were never structural — here, δ is the lever, and persona conditioning is the reduced-form fit. In psychometrics it is **construct validity** [Cronbach & Meehl 1955]: a measure is validated by behaving correctly across its nomological network, with dose-response as validity evidence a marginal cannot supply and **assay sensitivity** [ICH E10; Temple & Ellenberg 2000] naming the precondition our interiority gate enforces. In causal inference it is the **surrogate paradox** [Prentice 1989; VanderWeele 2013], now formalized for LLM outcomes [surrogacy framework 2026]. (Agent-based modeling's *equifinality* is the same trap at the aggregate-pattern level [Windrum et al. 2007; Grimm et al. 2005].)

**Contributions.** (1) The core result: a persona pool passes coarse marginal checks (band entry; variance comparability) while the δ comparative static stays ≤0.08 (§4.1). (2) Two identification statements naming what those checks cannot see: coarse condition-level bands only weakly partially identify the aggregate comparative static (Proposition A — with the identity Δ = μ₁ − μ₀ scoping the critique to coarse checks, since exact mean matching would force the effect); and neither aggregate moments nor complete condition marginals identify distribution shape or the cross-condition coupling Δᵢ (Proposition B) (§4.2). (3) A decomposed map of control-channel interactions: single-sentence switches, token–payoff conflicts, and the persona presence/direction dissociation (§4.3). (4) A methods lesson from a fully audited inference defect: the registered existence rule carried an estimated 12.9% false-fire probability under the audit null, was caught by external review — not the linter — and is republished with the audit and a rerun queued under a precommitted reporting rule (§4.4, A.7). (5) A working reliability protocol — procedurally exact, extensively auditable, inferentially fallible (§4.5, §6).

## 2. Related work

**Occupied territory, and where we sit.** Li & Ji [2026] establish, across 59,508 participants and 11 interventions, that LLM simulations can look right while causal effects go wrong — and study why prompting refinements that improve realism do not reliably improve effect accuracy. The surrogacy framework [2026] formalizes the assumptions under which LLM-outcome effects recover human effects; the "overly peaky or stereotyped" response profiles it notes in behavioral games are reported there as citations to prior empirical work [Gao et al. 2025], which corroborates our corner concentration from an independent lineage. Lin et al. [2026] identify a potentially overlapping failure mode: fixing an explicit persona does not ensure the model instantiates the same latent synthetic individual under different interventions. Our design rules out changes in explicit persona assignment, execution procedure, and environment randomization; it does not test latent-person invariance. What we directly identify is a condition-specific corner-mixture pattern in a fixed prompt panel; whether intervention-induced latent drift also contributes remains open, and the two mechanisms can coexist. Our differentiation: one concrete concealment mechanism, measured and decomposed, in incentive-bearing strategic interaction under prospective registration.

**LLM strategic behavior (direct collisions).** Akata et al. [2025] characterize LLM repeated-game play, modulable by prompt interventions; Pal, Mallela, Hilbe, Pracher, Wei, Fu, Schnell & Nowak [2026] elicit strategies of five models in repeated PD varying δ, payoffs, horizon knowledge, and framing, finding no model fully consistent; counterfactual-reasoning evaluations find label perturbations mild but payoff perturbations exposing canonical-strategy preservation [arXiv:2603.19167]; "strategic robustness" is defined as payoff-preserving invariance [arXiv:2607.19670]. These study bare single models. None construct persona pools, decompose marginal fidelity from response, run interiority-gated assays with family-level audits, or operate under mechanical adjudication — and our results qualify "modulable by prompts": prompts move marginals readily; the registered incentive-transmission rule fired exactly once, a result now held as a replication target after family audit (§4.4).

**Silicon-sampling critique lineage.** Bisbee et al. [2024] — our closest ally — find level match with compressed variance, distorted coefficients, and temporal drift in survey settings; Boelaert et al. [2025] report excess homogeneity; Anthis et al. [2025] catalog challenges; Hullman et al. [2026] argue for statistical calibration for confirmatory use (complementary: we address what must be *measured*). Park et al. [2024] show rich interview conditioning achieving strong individual-level replication — the strongest live candidate for a conditioning scheme that might pass response tests — our stated falsification prediction is that Park-style interview conditioning *does* materially improve response-matching, i.e., "cheap" is a property of lightweight trait personas, not of conditioning per se; one counterexample refutes sufficiency of coarse checks, and we do not estimate prevalence (odds stay out of the manuscript; A.5, resolved).

**Prompt sensitivity, personas, mechanism.** Format/phrasing sensitivity [Sclar et al. 2024; Brittlebench 2026]; role-play framing [Shanahan et al. 2023]; persona caricature/collapse [Lutz et al. 2025; arXiv:2604.24698]; persona practice implicitly assuming trait-fixed profiles (ICC≈1) where human expressed psychology runs ICC≈0.27 — 72–74% state — with LLMs "state-blind" to the difference [Harry et al., arXiv:2601.15395, verified] — our zero within-cell variation is that assumption made flesh. These lineages (collapse; state-blindness) occupy the general proposition that persona-conditioned diversity can be structurally hollow; our addition is connecting the hollowing to a specific failure of incentive-response validation in repeated strategic games, with a quantitative between/within decomposition under prospective registration. Also: human traits as density distributions of states [Fleeson 2001] versus point-masses; RLHF diversity reduction as the expected cause of the determinism [Kirk et al. 2024; Casper et al. 2023]. Full annotated map: `docs/analysis/literature-map.md`; differentiation table: `docs/analysis/novelty-relationships.md`.

## 3. The instrument

Subjects: gpt-4.1 (revision-pinned, verified at each phase entry), temperature 0.7, 16-token outputs, a fixed minimal behavioral-subject prompt (no game-theory vocabulary, no reasoning scaffold); Phase 5 prepends one sealed persona sentence per subject to byte-identical task text, giving every persona cell an exact bare twin. Cross-vendor tier: gemini-2.5-flash, descriptive; the original candidate, claude-haiku-4-5, **failed the registered behavioral entry gate** and was replaced under a registered, archived amendment — the failed round's full report is preserved in-repo. The gate failure is itself a methods finding: subject eligibility is empirical, not assumed. A model can fail to function as a behavioral subject — stable, parseable, non-degenerate play — before any experiment begins, and the amendment path preserved the failed round rather than discarding it. All environment randomness is seeded; every call is an event (hashed prompt, completion, decoding params, provenance), making the program byte-exact replayable with zero live calls (4,576/4,576 observations; verified anonymously post-publication). Claims are structured predicates registered before data, mechanically adjudicated in a fixed vocabulary; a two-sided interiority gate (CP95 on the registered counting unit wholly inside (0.05, 0.95); the seat-vs-episode counting question is disclosed below and queued as A.7-Q2) licenses slope inference and rejects floors *and* ceilings — a rule that prevented a false "δ-flat at ceiling" conclusion in Phase 4. Under-specifications discovered at adjudication were resolved by outcome-blind completions (four instances, each generating a new freeze-lint check). Behavioral drift sentinels with sealed baselines supervised both endpoints. The Phase 5 discussion was written as four pre-committed branches sealed before dispatch, with a registered selection rule.

The boundary of what this machinery guarantees, stated plainly: **the pipeline can enforce a registered predicate exactly; it cannot guarantee that the predicate represents a valid estimand, test family, or construct.** §4.4's family-error episode is the demonstration. Scale: ≈30,500 recorded subject calls, ≈13M input tokens, ≈US$15 of model spend at list prices; zero invalid outputs in Phases 4–5 (0/1,712 in Phase 5, including temperature 1.3).

## 4. Results

### 4.1 The core result: coarse marginal checks pass; the incentive comparative static stays weak

The trait-assigned leaning rule (≥2 of {agreeable, patient, risk-averse}) predicts round-1 cooperation with 0.5–0.7 gaps in every non-swap cell — persona direction is behaviorally enormous. Pool means (0.349–0.505 across the four repeated cells) enter the published human cooperation band in three of four cells; between-persona SDs (0.4241–0.4800) satisfied the registered variance-comparability criterion against the published human between-subject SDs (0.3116 and 0.4122). By the registered coarse criteria — band entry and variance comparability, evaluated per condition — the pool passes. One correction is owed before any matches-or-exceeds magnitude language returns: raw persona-mean SDs overstate true between-persona variance by finite-sample estimation noise (Var(p̂ᵢ) = Var(pᵢ) + E[pᵢ(1−pᵢ)/nᵢ]), and the human figures are not opportunity-matched; the hierarchical-binomial decomposition and matched comparison are registered as A.7-Q4/Q5 [PENDING].

Across the δ manipulation, the pool's round-1 cooperation moves ≤0.08 in every cell family. The published comparator [Dal Bó & Fréchette 2011] is **nonmatched** (different δ values, 0.50/0.75 vs our 0.10/0.90; different stage payoffs; monetary incentives; subjects who accumulate experience across supergames where our episodes are memory-less first exposures; its pooled experienced-data movement is ≈0.42 while its first-supergame ordering *reverses*), so no matched-magnitude claim appears anywhere in this paper and the figure stays out of the abstract; the load-bearing claim is internal: **the same pool that passes the registered marginal criteria fails to produce more than a small δ comparative static under matched, sealed, seed-controlled conditions.** A reanalysis of the DF replication microdata (first-exposure vs experienced views; per-subject endpoint mass; opportunity-count-matched variability) is scripted and pending the package download; its role is contextualization — the human per-subject response Δᵢ is unobservable in their between-session design, an asymmetry we state rather than paper over.

Interiority: 14 of 96 persona-cells are interior; ten of sixteen personas are pure-corner everywhere; every corner cell shows zero within-cell variation across all decisions. Two denominators describe interiority because two sets are registered: 96 is the descriptive census (16 personas × all 6 sealed task cells); 32 is the P5-1a restricted confirmatory set (16 personas × the 2 cells with exact bare twins), where the registered mixture predicate passed at 3/32 = 0.094 against θ₁ = 0.10 — by one unit. Two caveats attach to that verdict: we treat the continuous quantities (the census, the within-cell variation, the distributional comparisons) as primary and the binary verdict as secondary; and the interval counts seat-level trials while the declared independence unit is the episode, so an episode-clustered recomputation is registered with a precommitted reporting rule [PENDING A.7-Q2].

### 4.2 The mechanism, and what marginal checks cannot see

The pool's broad between-persona spread is a bimodal mixture of empirically corner-concentrated subjects. Two identification statements — design-level facts that name exactly what coarse checks cannot see, not free-standing theory (full statements and the binary-outcome variance decomposition μ(d), B(d), W(d), Δᵢ in `docs/analysis/propositions.md` [rewrite queued, A.7-Q3]):

**Proposition A (partial identification).** Suppose coarse validation certifies only |μ_d^S − μ_d^H| ≤ ε_d per condition d ∈ {0, 1} — band acceptance μ_d^S ∈ [ℓ_d, u_d]. Then the synthetic comparative static is constrained only to |Δ^S − Δ^H| ≤ ε₀ + ε₁, equivalently Δ^S ∈ [ℓ₁ − u₀, u₁ − ℓ₀]: an interval that can contain zero, the wrong sign, and magnitudes far from the human reference. Broad condition-level bands therefore only weakly partially identify the aggregate comparative static; they do not certify effect accuracy. The scoping identity is load-bearing: at ε₀ = ε₁ = 0, Δ = μ₁ − μ₀ forces the effect, so only *coarse* criteria are cheat-able — and coarse criteria are what the literature uses.

**Proposition B (shape and coupling, three layers).** (B1) Condition mean and total variance do not identify the between/within allocation, the propensity distribution F_d(p), or corner mass. (B2) Even exact (μ(d), B(d), W(d)) do not identify the *shape* of F_d(p): distributions sharing those quantities differ in skew, modality, and endpoint concentration. (B3) Even complete condition-specific marginals F₀ and F₁ do not identify the cross-condition coupling F₀₁, hence not the distribution of per-subject responses Δᵢ = pᵢ(1) − pᵢ(0); Fréchet–Hoeffding bounds delimit what marginals alone permit, and only repeated-measures or structural assumptions go further. *Latent-invariance caveat:* reusing an explicit persona prompt across conditions supplies a prompt-indexed coupling Δᵢ^prompt; reading it as a stable synthetic individual's potential-outcome contrast requires a latent-invariance assumption our design does not test [Lin et al. 2026]. Human panels and corner mixtures can be moment-equivalent; only within-subject designs or response-surface tests separate them.

The corner concentration itself is expected, not anomalous: RLHF reduces output diversity toward mode collapse [Kirk et al. 2024], and independent work reports "overly peaky or stereotyped" prompted response profiles in behavioral games [surrogacy framework 2026]. What our design adds is the demonstration that pooling such subjects *manufactures* the marginal signatures validators check.

### 4.3 Control-channel interactions

The bare subject: round-1 cooperation 0.000 at every δ (320 decisions; policy-level CP upper bounds ≈0.14–0.17); a single continuation sentence (S2, localized by a span-ladder and confirmed on fresh seeds) flips it to 0.925 (37/40); the identical wording factor is null in one-shot play (+0.006) — switches conduct only where context gives them referents. In the label-swap conflict cell (canonical payoffs, "Cooperate" on the defecting action), the bare subject chooses the cooperation-worded option 0/40 — taking the strictly dominated action whenever it carries "Defect" — while responding to matrix changes when labels do not oppose them (+0.788). The precise claim is therefore **conditional dominance**: semantic cues can override payoff dominance in conflict cells; payoff sensitivity is representation-conditional, not absent. The label-swap result is counterintuitive enough that prose invites disbelief; the schematic:

| Cell | Underlying action | Displayed label | Stage-payoff status | Bare-subject choice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aligned | defect | "Defect" | strictly dominant | tracks matrix contrasts (+0.788) |
| Aligned | cooperate | "Cooperate" | dominated | — |
| Swap | defect | "Cooperate" | strictly dominant | chosen 0/40 |
| Swap | cooperate | "Defect" | dominated | chosen 40/40 |

In the swap cell the bare subject follows the token, not the payoff: the strictly dominated action is selected whenever it carries "Defect."

Personas add two separable forces. *Direction* drives the 0.5–0.7 leaning gaps. *Presence* flips the lexical attractor in the swap cell: all sixteen personas — both leanings, refusal shares 0.683–1.000 — choose the cooperation-worded/payoff-dominant option where the bare subject chose the opposite 40/40. Because word and payoff coincide there, the mechanism (incentive response vs prosocial-word attraction) is unidentified; the de-confounding cell (balanced payoffs × persona) is scope-sealed to future work, and this figure never appears without this caveat. The registered dominance predicate returned persona-dominant (pooled task-consistent share 0.128, CP [0.104, 0.155]; the authors' registered "task-text dominates" failed) — but the pooled figure is carried by the confounded swap cell; the confound-free repeated subset runs 0.234 task-consistent ("mixed" at the pinned bound), and the cleanest payoff-opposed slice (cooperative-leaning personas vs S2-absent wording *and* the dominant strategy) is 65% persona-consistent. Identity contests and usually beats the task layer; formal dominance holds only pooled. We describe these as **control-channel interactions**, not a hierarchy: precedence is cell-dependent.

### 4.4 A registered candidate, downgraded on audit (secondary)

One persona passed the registered interiority-gated slope assay: **p13** ("You are Harper, a 61-year-old landscape gardener. People who know you describe you as competitive, patient, and risk-averse."), interior at both S2-absent δ levels (0.333 → 0.750), one-sided 95% slope LB +0.083 [PENDING A.7-Q2 on the gate interval]. Three candidates passed the gate (p04/s2p, p05/s2a, p13/s2a); p13 was the only gate-passing candidate with a positive slope whose registered lower bound exceeded zero — the earlier "only interior δ-pair" phrasing was imprecise and is retired. The post-adjudication family audit ran over the 32 evaluable clause-(a) surfaces (of 144 registered-eligible; clause (b) was audited separately under Bonferroni) and gave p = 0.0525 ± 0.0050 at B = 2,000. Two defects keep that number out of the abstract regardless of its value: Monte Carlo uncertainty at B = 2,000 spans both sides of 0.05, and the audit substituted a Newcombe lower bound for the registered BCa bound — a valid audit of an approximating procedure, not an exact audit of the registered one. A rerun is registered with a precommitted reporting rule (A.7-Q1): an exactly recomputable statistic (T_max over gated slope z-scores), B ≥ 100,000 or sequential Monte Carlo to a fixed CI half-width, p̂ = (r+1)/(B+1) with a binomial interval, and the family definition (32 vs 56 vs 144) adjudicated by the reviewer panel *before* compute (A.5-2). Status: **replication target, not confirmatory evidence.** The mechanical verdict stands as history; the inferential status is downgraded — the program's first post-adjudication claim-status downgrade, a category distinct from the twelve data-refuted predictions — and a new rule is registered going forward: every predicate declares its family-level error control at freeze. The exploratory patience/factor-tension reading moves to A.1. ## 5. Discussion

### 5.1 What follows

For synthetic-participant practice: coarse marginal fidelity is a reduced-form fit, achievable by a one-sentence identity prepend, and its failure mode is silent — a researcher using this pool would have replicated human cooperation levels and concluded the shadow of the future barely matters, with clean-looking data. Validation should move to response surfaces: preregistered comparative-static predicates against pinned (and explicitly matched-or-not) human quantities, through assay-sensitivity gates, per (model, prompt registry, provider route, time-window) — the last coordinate demonstrated real by the drift record. Statistical calibration [Hullman et al. 2026] and surrogacy assumptions [2026] are complementary formal routes; Lin et al.'s [2026] drift diagnostics probe a mechanism our design leaves open, and belong in any follow-up arm (A.3). For deployment: strategic behavior rewritable by one sentence or one identity line, with context-dependent channel precedence, is a safety-relevant property demonstrated rather than argued.

## 6. Limitations

Confirmatory results rest on one model deployment; n = 16 at the persona level; the persona-population estimand is weakly powered by design. The P5-3 mechanism is unidentified for 15/16 passes (word/payoff confound; de-confounding cell unrun by scope-seal). The family-error defect in the registered clause-(a) rule was caught by external review, not the linter — semantic and inferential completeness are not fully mechanizable, and the instance ledger (22 process instances → 6 rules) documents preregistration-under-automation degrading gracefully rather than perfectly. The human comparator is published and nonmatched; the microdata reanalysis is pending; human Δᵢ is unobservable in the comparator's between-session design regardless. P5-1a passed by one unit. The entropy-vs-temperature inversion (supplement) lacks a mechanism. The δ × high-temperature interaction is non-estimable by registered aliasing. Gemini-tier results are descriptive under documented endpoint non-stationarity. Latent-person invariance across conditions is untested: sealed templates and matched seeds control explicit assignment and procedure, not the model's implicit completion of a fixed persona [Lin et al. 2026]. Several registered intervals count seat-level trials while the declared independence unit is the episode; episode-clustered sensitivities are queued (A.7-Q2) and the affected verdicts are marked pending them. Raw between-persona SDs include finite-sample estimation noise; the corrected decomposition is queued (A.7-Q4).
