## Downsampling note

The downsampled columns re-estimate each subject from k=12 opportunities (matching our per-persona seat-trial count per cell), seeded; this shows how much of the human endpoint mass and spread survives at OUR n — the honest comparison scale for any distribution-shape statement. A hierarchical (beta-binomial) separation of subject variance from finite-sample noise is the natural next step once the real package is in place; it is deliberately not fitted to the fixture.
