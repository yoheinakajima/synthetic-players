## The two estimands

1. **Conditional finite-panel estimand** — properties of *these 16 fixed persona prompts* (and the bare subject), treated as a fixed panel. All registered Phase 5 predicates (P5-1a, P5-1b, P5-2, P5-3, P5-4) attach HERE: personas enter as fixed units, and no claim generalizes beyond the panel.
2. **Persona-population estimand** — properties of the trait-persona *generator* (the composition rule the 16 were drawn from), for which the panel is a sample of n=16 at the persona level. Only the exploratory layers (leaning splits, trait-gradient sketches, between/within decompositions read as generator properties) gesture at THIS estimand, and n=16 with 3 binary factors leaves it weakly identified — every such reading is banner-labeled exploratory.

Any sentence in the paper claiming 'persona pools do X' must state which estimand it attaches to; the registered record supports only the first.
